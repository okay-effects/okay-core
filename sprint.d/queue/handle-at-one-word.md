# handle-at-one-word

`handle(h)(body)` takes the facade's `Handler` on `!`; `handleAt(h)(body)` the kernel's `Passing`/`Stateful` at
any answers — two words for one act. `Handler.at` already gives `OneAnswering` for an `Answering`: `handle` could
dispatch by the static type of `h`, as the kernel's `handle` does (`inline`, `summonFrom`): a `Handler` whose `at`
is `Passing` at any answers, else on `!` — `handleAt` then a private road. With linear-sugar. Half a day.
