# guard-at-answers

A guard under a `$`, at any answers: `ensure` and `uncancelable` inside a program moving the answer (`Atm`), a
`shift0` passing the guard, its `k` holding it — the finalizer running at every resumption, as a `Stateful`'s state
is carried. Not `recover`: it replaces the level's value with a program of effects, at no answers — typed only when
the answer is unchanged over the level (`J = O`); stays on `!`.

The road is `Through`'s: `Guarded` with the answers (`k: Frame[T, X, I, A, J]`, `body: Cont[T, I, O, X]`,
`Cont[T, J, O, B]`), `Meta.UnderGuard` with `J`/`O` for `N`, the six sites in the machine (entry, `outE`, `shifted`
— `never` today — `unwind`, `resumed`, `failed`); `TestReference`: a guard under the `$` in the generator and in
`NormC`; the facade's `guard`/`ensure`/`uncancelable` on `Cont[R, I, O, A]`. About a day; eval's size unchanged (the
cases are there, the types move).

Wanted only when a program opens and closes a resource between a `shift0` and its `$`; until one does, the guard
outside the `$` — `Atm.dollar(body)(ret).ensure(fin)` — is the way, and the `$` the resource's boundary. Noted
2026-10-14 after the facade's `Atm`; not picked.
