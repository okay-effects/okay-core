# linear-sugar

The facade's `Handler` is `Deep` by `Handler.at`: a user's clause that calls `k` once and at once still takes the
rest as data. A word for the kernel's `Linear` (`k` once, with the operation, the answers through) and for
`Translating` beside `translate` — `handleLinear(h)(body)` or a `Handler` subclass `Linear` with its own `at`
— so a logger or an interceptor resuming at once costs no `k`. API, not a lane: no benchmark takes this road
(mixed-abort-road says why); wanted when a user's handler shows up in a profile.
